## Classification and contribution
The locked final book is a **reviewed educational book**, not a dataset and not an empirical research paper. Do not reopen book review. The separately curated candidate tables are **nonempirical educational/reference annotations and prospective evaluation designs** derived from music-theory examples; they are neither observed datasets nor a validated machine-learning benchmark. Reusable contribution: structured chord/event specifications, instructional cases, and documentation, *subject to row-level source and musical checks*. The musical theory is established prior art; broad scientific novelty is not demonstrated. Potential beneficiaries: musicians, educators and research teams investigating interpretable musical specifications. No model evaluation, experimental results or verified audio adherence have been collected.

## Harvard-specific eligibility
The **Harvard Dataverse Repository** is Harvard's hosted research-data repository; **Dataverse** also refers to the broader software platform. Harvard says it checks administrative usability and research-data scope, not peer review or scientific quality. The book alone is **not eligible as a research dataset**. A well-curated, independently useful educational reference dataset *may* meet the research-data scope if the content, methodology, variables, provenance and reuse proposition can be substantiated; eligibility is not yet established. Do not invent observations or experiments to qualify.

Harvard permits researchers outside Harvard to deposit in principle and recommends creation of a Dataverse Collection, but **the author's account, existing collection, contributor permission and specific institutional collection are UNKNOWN**. Personal/researcher collection is a proposed route, not a confirmed account capability. No external collection affiliation is asserted.
