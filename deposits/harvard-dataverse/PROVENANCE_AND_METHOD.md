## Reproducibility boundary
A reader may reproduce symbolic calculations using stated notes, keys, measure numbers and harmony. Actual audio generation cannot be reproduced without a selected model/version, prompts, settings, source audio and logged outputs; those are **not supplied**. Blank templates only describe prospective workflows.
