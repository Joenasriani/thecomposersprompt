**Proposed dataset title:** *Music-Theory Harmonic Specification Examples from The Composer’s Prompt: Curated Educational Reference Data*.

**Creator:** Joe Nasr (same person as rights holder Joseph Ribal Nasr); **year:** 2026; **subject:** Arts and Humanities; **keywords:** music theory; tonal harmony; chord progressions; harmonic function; music education; computational music; symbolic music; AI-assisted composition. **Contact:** to be supplied by depositor (do not invent). **Collection:** to be selected in authenticated Harvard Dataverse interface (no Harvard affiliation implied).

**Description:** This nonempirical reference dataset records authored, written music-theory specifications derived from the reviewed 159-page educational book *The Composer’s Prompt*. The presently in-repository subset comprises one eight-bar C-major reference, six eight-bar compositional cases and forty-eight corresponding chord-position records. It contains prescribed chords and notation-related metadata, not observed or generated audio, participant responses, model runs, measured accuracy or independently established predictive claims. It may support teaching, reproducible symbolic comparisons and future evaluation designs. The book itself is a separately copyrighted free-to-read publication and is not distributed as part of this dataset. The rest of the historical candidate corpus remains outside the proposed verified subset.
