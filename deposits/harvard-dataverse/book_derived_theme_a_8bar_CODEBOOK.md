# Codebook — directly sourced Theme A miniature reference

**Scope:** One eight-bar symbolic teaching example sourced from final reviewed 159-page *The Composer’s Prompt*, chapter 10 §§10.1–10.2. This file is an **illustrative educational annotation, not empirical research data, a trained model benchmark, or proof of audio generation**. Its existence alone does not establish Harvard eligibility.

Fields: `example_id` (constant local identifier); `bar` (1–8, sequential integer); `chord` (written chord symbols); `key_center` (C major); `meter` (4/4); `quarter_note_bpm` (96); `roman_numeral` (harmonic degree in C major); `source_locator` (chapter/section in final book); `evidence_type` (`book_specification`, not observed sound).

Written sequence: C | G | Am | F | C | F | G | C; Roman numerals I | V | vi | IV | I | IV | V | I. One chord per bar. Bars 7–8 are a **candidate** V–I cadence gesture; no performed cadence is evidenced. Chapter 10 supplies these values directly. 8 × 4 quarter beats at quarter=96 BPM gives a notated duration of 20 seconds without pauses or rubato.
