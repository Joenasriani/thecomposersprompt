# Music-theory harmonic specification examples — Harvard candidate

**Type:** nonempirical curated educational reference **Dataset** (Dataverse publication object type), not empirical observations or a peer-reviewed research publication. **Status: NOT READY, NOT DEPOSITED.**

## What data are present
A small book-derived symbolic data subset: eight-bar Theme A; six authored comparison cases; 48 written chord-position targets. Source: *The Composer’s Prompt* (Joe Nasr, 2026), chapter 10 and the relevant harmonic transformation chapters. The final reviewed 159-page PDF is a **reference** and is not being reissued here. The file descriptions and boundaries are in `FILE_LEVEL_CLASSIFICATION.csv`; the variable definitions for Theme A are in `book_derived_theme_a_8bar_CODEBOOK.md`. Future sound or AI evaluation is only prospective.

## Methods and limitations
Each record is a symbolic written specification, not an audio transcription. The 48 event rows map eight positions in each of six written cases. The separate source-check report documents the bounded checks performed and areas not independently validated. Do not treat these cases as a population sample, general music theory benchmark, or evidence of model adherence. The wider private draft corpus and uninspected second-book records are outside this release subset.
