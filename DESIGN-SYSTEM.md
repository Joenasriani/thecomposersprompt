# Design System – Specification and Decision Register

**Status:** DIRECTION LOCKED; DETAILED SYSTEM NOT YET DESIGNED OR APPROVED.  
**Authority:** [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) Stage 1 acceptance gate.

## Intended identity
A contemporary interactive music publication and precision-designed creative instrument. The interface must be recognizable through **real musical structure, meaningful interaction and editorial control**, rather than through standard course templates or ornament.

## Experience architecture
- **Explore:** musical laboratories where sound, notation and visuals express the same underlying note/chord state.
- **Read:** uninterrupted, searchable manuscript experience with compact contents, clear chapter progression and readable theory.
- **Compose:** purposeful workspace to specify, copy, listen, note observations and revise compositions.

Each experience gets context-appropriate controls; never put the whole manuscript menu permanently beside every interaction on a narrow screen.

## Locked creative constraints
1. Avoid generic card grids, oversized prose introductions, default-colored buttons and repetitive filler sections.
2. Musical graphics earn their place by communicating pitch, interval, rhythm, tension or structure. Do not simulate interactions.
3. Use musical notation, geometry, timing and silence as meaningful design vocabulary.
4. Keep primary interaction obvious; reveal advanced controls progressively.
5. Minimize reading friction and navigation density, particularly on phones.
6. Protect musical correctness: visible notes, sounding notes and stated harmony must agree.
7. Provide reduced-motion, keyboard and non-WebGL alternatives.
8. Maintain one coherent visual system across Explore, Read and Compose.
9. Favor restrained, authored composition rather than decorative Three.js motion.
10. Do not reuse the existing merged editorial prototype as an approved visual system.

## To design and approve in Stage 1
- Color tokens and contrast, typography and typographic scale, grid, rhythm and whitespace.
- Navigation and information architecture for 42 sections and 11 rooms.
- Interactive instrument/control pattern library and musical feedback vocabulary.
- Motion constraints, breakpoints, responsive keyboard/score treatment.
- Three.js / Tone.js / VexFlow module boundaries, loading/fallbacks and verified license notices.
- Functional desktop/mobile specimen and acceptance screenshots.

**No specific palette, template, motion language or component library is approved yet.** Proposed choices must be evaluated in a working prototype.
