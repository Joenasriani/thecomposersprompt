## Acceptance gates
1. Validate all 42 manuscript entries, including Appendices A–F.
2. Verify existing practice rooms, playback, keyboard interaction, prompt copying and reading progress.
3. Check layout at 320, 375, 768, 1024 and 1440 pixels, including mobile keyboard scrolling and no horizontal page overflow.
4. Compare manuscript against the final approved book PDF, subsection by subsection, **not yet verified**.
5. Compare against late historical approved versions and user notes, **not yet verified**.
6. Merge and production deploy only after explicit approval.

This is a preservation checkpoint, not a claim of full feature-parity verification or completion of new chapter content.
