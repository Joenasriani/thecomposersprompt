Chapter 6: Key and Mode as Containers

6.1 The Key Declaration

The single most important Theory 101 prompt move is declaring the key in the Style box and reinforcing it with bracketed chords in the lyrics. Suno does not natively read chord symbols like a lead-sheet musician; if chord names appear without brackets, the model may literally sing them as lyrics. Bracketed chords—[Am] before a lyric line—function as structural harmony instructions, and pairing them with an explicit key declaration ("A minor key" in the Style box) "sharply improves the hit rate" and keeps the model within the correct scale collection (Am, Bdim, C, Dm, Em, F, G for A natural minor).

Diagnostic pair — with and without key declaration:

Weak (no key):

Style: "Melancholic indie-folk, acoustic guitar, 90 BPM, warm male vocals" Lyrics: [Am] City sleeps and [F] I am wide awake / [C] Streetlights at the [G] window tremble soft

Strong (key declared):

Style: "Melancholic indie-folk, acoustic guitar, 90 BPM, warm male vocals, A minor key" Lyrics: (identical)

Four more key-declaration examples across genres:

Example A — Major key, pop:

Style: "Upbeat pop, 120 BPM, C major key, bright piano, four-on-the-floor drums, female vocals" Lyrics: [C] … [G] … [Am] … [F]

Example B — Minor key, rock:

Style: "Hard rock, 140 BPM, E minor key, distorted guitars, driving drums, aggressive male vocals" Lyrics: [Em] … [C] … [G] … [D]

Example C — Modal, Dorian:

Style: "Fusion jazz, 132 BPM, D Dorian mode, electric piano, fretless bass, syncopated drums" Lyrics: [Dm7] … [G7] … [Dm7] … [G7]

Example D — Modal, Mixolydian:

Style: "Classic rock, 110 BPM, A Mixolydian mode, open guitar chords, tambourine, gritty vocals" Lyrics: [A] … [G] … [D] … [A]

6.2 The Singing-Chords Failure Mode

Without brackets, Suno may literally sing the chord names.

Prompt (failure mode):

Lyrics: "Am F G Em, walking through the rain tonight"

Output: Suno sings "A minor, F, G, E minor" as lyrics instead of playing them instrumentally.

Prompt (corrected):

Lyrics: [Am] Walking through the rain tonight [F] Shadows falling left and right

Additional failure-mode examples:

Failure: "C G Am F, I remember summer" Corrected: [C] I remember [G] summer [Am] fading [F] slow

Failure: "E minor, C major, G major, D major" Corrected: [Em] … [C] … [G] … [D]

The lesson is that the prompt's syntax is not a neutral container; it is a semantic signal.

Dorian:

Style: "Jazz fusion, Dorian mode in D, altered scale over dominant chords, comping with shell voicings and extensions, 120 BPM" Lyrics: [Dm7] … [G7] … [Dm7] … [G7]

Style: "Flamenco-influenced rock, E Phrygian mode, nylon-string guitar, palmas, 95 BPM" Lyrics: [Em] … [F] … [Em] … [F]

Lydian:

Style: "Film-score orchestral, F Lydian mode, sweeping strings, harp, celestial choir, 70 BPM" Lyrics: [Fmaj7] … [G] … [Fmaj7] … [G]

Mixolydian:

Style: "Southern rock, G Mixolydian mode, slide guitar, Hammond organ, 100 BPM" Lyrics: [G] … [F] … [C] … [G]

Altered scale:

Style: "Modern jazz, altered scale over G7, saxophone solo, walking bass, ride cymbal, 160 BPM" Lyrics: [G7alt] … [Cmaj7]

Aeolian:

Style: "Gothic rock, A Aeolian mode, chorus guitar, driving bass, 130 BPM" Lyrics: [Am] … [F] … [G] … [Em]

6.4 Forcing Non-Major Modes

Never rely solely on "Dorian" or "maqam Hijaz." Pair the mode name with concrete anchors: root + interval sequence, reference piece ("like Miles Davis's 'So What'"), or MIDI clip.

For Hijaz maqam, the full specification:

"Hijaz maqam on D: D–E#–F–G–A–Bb–C–D; emphasize E#–F and A–Bb neighbor motion; texture: solo oud; tempo 66 BPM, 10/8; no Western harmony—only heterophonic variation"

The same principle applies to modal jazz:

"Dorian on D, intervals [0,2,3,5,7,9,10], emphasize minor 7th, texture: Rhodes + muted guitar, 90 BPM, no major cadences"

The interval array [0,2,3,5,7,9,10] is a machine-readable specification of the Dorian scale (with 0 = D). The phrase "no major cadences" is a negative constraint that prevents the model from defaulting to V–I resolution. Together, they are more reliable than the bare word "Dorian."
