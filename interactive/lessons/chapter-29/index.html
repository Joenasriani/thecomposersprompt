29.1 Musical Briefs and Experimental Tags

Use the style description to state genre, instrumentation, tempo, key and arrangement intentions. Bracketed chord symbols and labels such as [Modulation] in the examples below are experimental requests, not guaranteed commands. Check whether they help in your selected model and input field.

Product controls should be set through the interface or a documented API. Do not assume that parameter-like words pasted into a prompt change a setting. See chapter 24 for the verified Creative Sliders reference.

29.2 Version and Feature Claims

The original model-specific duration, stem-count, bitrate and editing claims have been withdrawn from this teaching edition pending verification against current product documentation. They are retained in the original wording below for traceability.

Production descriptions such as “balanced EQ” express a preference. They do not guarantee a measurable mastering result or replace audio inspection.

Chapter 29: Suno

29.1 Core Syntax

  • Style box: Key, BPM, genre, instrumentation, mood, production texture
  • Lyrics: Bracketed chords ([Am]) across lyric lines; section brackets ([Verse], [Chorus]); production notes
  • Advanced tags: [Chords: |Dm9|-|G13|-|Cmaj7|]; [Secondary dominant]; [Modulation]
  • Parameters: negative_tags, style_weight, weirdness_constraint, vocal_gender, persona_id

29.2 Version-Specific Features

  • Suno v5/v5.5: Full songs with vocals, up to 8 minutes, 12 stems, 320 kbps audio
  • Suno v6: Section-level inpainting; stem separation (Auto Split + Advanced Split with nearly 100 instrument categories); Studio for multitrack editing
  • Mastering prompts: "Balanced EQ, precise compression, professional stereo imaging"; "automation for expressive changes"; "vocal processing including EQ, compression, reverb, and delay"
