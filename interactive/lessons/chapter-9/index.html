9.3 The Voice-Leading Field Test

Voice leading is "the linear progression of individual melodic lines and their interaction to create harmonies, typically in accordance with principles of common-practice harmony and counterpoint."

Two core rules that survive into jazz, pop, and AI:

  1. Move each voice the shortest distance possible. Retain common tones, move by step.
  2. Avoid parallel fifths and octaves to keep voices independent.

AI often fails here: "bass leaps awkwardly, tritone resolution vanishes, dominant function collapses."

The fastest theory check: Teach students to test AI output by playing the chords in root position, then in the closest inversion. If three or more voices leap more than a third, functional intent is absent. This is the quickest way to diagnose whether the model has produced a functional progression or merely a sequence of chord labels.

Prompt implementations for voice leading:

  • "smooth bass, stepwise, retain common tones"
  • "resolve 7ths down, leading tones up"
  • "close voicing, avoid parallel fifths"
  • "inversion C/E for stepwise bass"
