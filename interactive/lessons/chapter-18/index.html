Chapter 18: Parallel Chords and Harmonic Color

18.1 The Impressionist Break from Functional Voice Leading

In traditional voice leading, parallel fifths and octaves are prohibited. Debussy and Ravel broke this rule deliberately: they treated chords as units that could be moved in parallel motion—"planing"—weakening the tonic-fixing function of harmony and treating the chord as a color medium independent of functional progression.
