19.3 AIVA's Roman Numeral Rule

For AIVA specifically, use roman numerals, not letters—I, V, vi, IV generates faster and more accurately than C - G - Am - F. This is a tool-specific syntactic rule, not a general principle. The theory is the same; the encoding differs.
