22.1 The Pre-Flight Checklist

Project brief:

"A 3-minute indie-folk song about leaving a hometown. Influences: Bon Iver, Phoebe Bridgers. Mood: bittersweet but hopeful. Vocal: intimate female, close-miked. Production: warm, organic, minimal."

Derived specification:

  • Key: D major (bright but with modal borrowing)
  • Tempo: 88 BPM
  • Structure: Intro–Verse 1–Chorus 1–Verse 2–Chorus 2–Bridge–Final Chorus–Outro
  • Harmonic plan:
    • Verse: I–V–vi–IV (D–A–Bm–G)
    • Chorus: IV–I–V–vi (G–D–A–Bm)
    • Bridge: modal interchange to B minor (relative), ♭VI and ♭VII borrowed
  • Orchestration: fingerpicked guitar, muted piano, brushed drums, cello, ambient textures
  • Mix intent: intimate, vocal-forward, warm analog

22.2 Structural-Level Thinking

Prolongational prompt example:

"Compose a 32-bar passage in C major with a structural I–V–I prolongation. The surface harmony should elaborate the tonic (I) in bars 1–8 with vi and IV, prolong the dominant (V) in bars 17–24 with ii and IV, and return to tonic (I) in bars 25–32 with an authentic cadence. Use passing chromaticism (secondary dominants, borrowed chords) as surface elaboration."
