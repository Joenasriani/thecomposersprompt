Chapter 21: The Timeline, Not the Equipment List

21.1 From Instruments to Behavior

The decisive shift at Level 4 is from describing what instruments are present to specifying what each instrument is doing, when it enters, and how the texture changes over time.

21.2 Timeline Prompt Examples

Post-rock build:

Style: "Cinematic post-rock, 95 BPM, D minor, building from solo piano to full orchestra" [Verse 1 – solo piano, sustain pedal] [Dm] … [B♭] … [F] … [C] [Chorus 1 – add cello and viola, arco] [Dm] … [B♭] … [F] … [C] [Bridge – full strings, timpani roll, crescendo] [Gm] … [A7] … [Dm] … [Dm]

Electronic build:

Style: "Progressive house, 128 BPM, G minor, classic club build" [Intro – kick and filtered pad only] [Gm] … [E♭] … [B♭] … [F] [Build – add snare roll and riser, filter opens] [Gm] … [E♭] … [B♭] … [F] [Drop – full kick, sidechained bass, plucked lead] [Gm] … [E♭] … [B♭] … [F]

Country arrangement:

Style: "Modern country, 92 BPM, G major, radio-ready" [Verse – acoustic guitar and bass only] [G] … [C] … [G] … [D] [Chorus – add full band, pedal steel, harmonies] [G] … [C] … [G] … [D] [Bridge – drop to acoustic, build with fiddle] [Em] … [C] … [G] … [D] [Final Chorus – full band, double-time feel] [G] … [C] … [G] … [D]

Orchestral crescendo:

Style: "Romantic orchestral, 66 BPM, C minor, long crescendo" [Opening – solo oboe, pp] [Cm] … [A♭] … [Fm] … [G7] [Middle – strings enter, mp] [Cm] … [A♭] … [Fm] … [G7] [Climax – full orchestra, fff] [Cm] … [A♭] … [Fm] … [G7]

21.3 Udio Prose Description

Orchestral prose:

"strings in pianissimo, harp arpeggiating, horns and clarinets introduce the theme, full orchestra in fortissimo with brass and tremolo strings"

Rock prose:

"verse is just acoustic guitar and close-miked vocals, pre-chorus adds bass and kick drum, chorus opens up with distorted guitars and crash cymbals, bridge drops to piano and strings"

Electronic prose:

"intro is filtered pad and hi-hats only, then the kick enters with the bass, then the lead synth arpeggio comes in at the drop, then the breakdown strips everything back to the vocal and pad"

21.4 A Comprehensive Arrangement Specification

Specification document:

  • Structure: 32 bars, 4 sections of 8 bars each
  • Texture states:
    1. State A (bars 1–8): solo instrument, low density
    2. State B (bars 9–16): add rhythm section, medium density
    3. State C (bars 17–24): full ensemble, high density
    4. State D (bars 25–32): drop to sparse texture, then final build
  • Instrument entries/exits:
    • Piano: bars 1–32 (continuous)
    • Bass: bars 9–32
    • Drums: bars 13–32
    • Strings: bars 17–28, return at 30
    • Brass: bars 25–32
  • Energy curve: low → medium → high → low → very high

Diagnostic test: Write an arrangement timeline for a 32-bar structure with at least four distinct textural states. Compile it to a prompt and generate. Map the generated audio's energy curve against the specified timeline.
