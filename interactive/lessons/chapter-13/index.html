Chapter 13: The ARR-SPEC Method

13.1 Specification Before Generation

The ARR-SPEC model formalizes Level 2. A brief becomes a document specifying key, tempo, bar-accurate section map, chord letters and Roman numerals, who enters and leaves when, the energy curve, vocal delivery, and mix intent—then compiles that document into a Suno prompt.

13.2 Worked ARR-SPEC Examples

ARR-SPEC #1 — Indie-rock:

Specification:

  • Key: E minor
  • Tempo: 92 BPM
  • Section map:
    • Verse 1 (bars 1–8): [Em] [C] [G] [D] — establish tonic, half cadence on D
    • Pre-chorus (bars 9–16): [Am] [B7] [Em] [C] — build dominant tension, authentic cadence on Em
    • Chorus (bars 17–24): [G] [D] [Em] [C] — resolve to relative major, energy peak
    • Bridge (bars 25–32): [Am] [F] [C] [G] — modal interchange to A minor, no cadence (open)
  • Energy curve: low → medium → high → low → very high

Suno prompt:

Style: Melancholic indie-rock, 92 BPM, E minor key, fingerpicked acoustic guitar, full band, strings, warm male vocals Lyrics: [Verse 1] [Em] … [C] … [G] … [D] (half cadence) [Pre-Chorus] [Am] … [B7] … [Em] … [C] (authentic cadence) [Chorus] [G] … [D] … [Em] … [C] [Bridge] [Am] … [F] … [C] … [G] (open)

ARR-SPEC #2 — Synthwave:

Specification:

  • Key: F# minor
  • Tempo: 110 BPM
  • Section map:
    • Intro (bars 1–8): [F#m] [D] [A] [E] — establish groove, filtered
    • Verse (bars 9–24): same progression, vocals enter
    • Chorus (bars 25–40): [D] [A] [E] [F#m] — rotate progression, full synths
    • Bridge (bars 41–48): [Bm] [D] [A] [E] — lift to relative major, no drums
    • Final chorus (bars 49–64): return to chorus progression, double-time drums
  • Energy curve: medium → medium → high → low → very high

Suno prompt:

Style: Synthwave, 110 BPM, F# minor key, analog synth arpeggios, gated reverb drums, warm bass, retro 80s production Lyrics: [Intro] [F#m] … [D] … [A] … [E] [Verse] [F#m] … [D] … [A] … [E] [Chorus] [D] … [A] … [E] … [F#m] [Bridge] [Bm] … [D] … [A] … [E] [Final Chorus] [D] … [A] … [E] … [F#m]

ARR-SPEC #3 — Jazz standard:

Specification:

  • Key: B♭ major
  • Tempo: 140 BPM (medium swing)
  • Section map:
    • A1 (bars 1–8): [B♭maj7] [Gm7] [Cm7] [F7] — I–vi–ii–V
    • A2 (bars 9–16): same, with variation
    • B (bars 17–24): [E♭maj7] [Cm7] [F7] [B♭maj7] — IV–ii–V–I
    • A3 (bars 25–32): return with ii–V–I cadence
  • Energy curve: medium throughout, with improvisational peaks

Suno prompt:

Style: Jazz trio, 140 BPM, B♭ major key, swing feel, piano comping, walking upright bass, ride cymbal, ii–V–I progressions Lyrics: [A1] [B♭maj7] … [Gm7] … [Cm7] … [F7] [A2] [B♭maj7] … [Gm7] … [Cm7] … [F7] [B] [E♭maj7] … [Cm7] … [F7] … [B♭maj7] [A3] [B♭maj7] … [Gm7] … [Cm7] … [F7] … [B♭maj7]

13.3 Diagnostic Test

Compare the energy curve of the output to the specified curve. Where did the model flatten a build or rush a cadence? Revise the section-local production notes, not the global genre tags.

Part IV: Level 3 — Color and Chromaticism (Modern Harmony)

Level 2 specified the phrase's harmonic job, cadential target, and melodic embellishment. Level 3 adds a new grain of precision: color. The diatonic skeleton is now dressed with extensions and alterations, chromatic borrowings, localized tonicizations, specific voicings, and parallel motion.
