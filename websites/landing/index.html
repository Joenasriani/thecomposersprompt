The Composer’s Prompt

Music Theory and Harmony for Computational Music Prompting

Music theory becomes a way to specify compositional choices. Learn how to describe tonal centers, modes, chords, timing, melodic movement, arrangement and form, then evaluate whether generated audio follows the musical plan.

From theory to a musical specification

Work through intervals, scales, modes, tonal harmony, progressions, voice leading, harmonic rhythm and musical form. Convert these concepts into structured musical instructions instead of vague stylistic descriptions.

Specify, generate, evaluate

Write prompts with testable musical targets. Compare the requested result with what is actually heard, and distinguish a written specification from independently verified audio.
