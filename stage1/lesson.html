CHAPTER 6.5 / MUSIC THEORY AND HARMONY

From scales to chords

A chord begins with notes. Learn how to build a major or minor triad, then hear the difference by changing just one pitch.

01 · CONSTRUCT

Build a chord from a scale

The C-major scale contains C D E F G A B. Start on C and select every other note: C–E–G. These are the root, major third and perfect fifth.

Change only E to E♭ and the chord becomes C minor. The root and perfect fifth stay the same.

Major third: E is 4 semitones above C. The perfect fifth G is 7 semitones above C.
